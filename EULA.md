## Before distribution or Pro sales

The following is a maintainer checklist, not additional end-user terms:

- Have counsel review ownership, contributor permissions, employment-related
  rights, and the provenance and protectability of AI-assisted code and assets,
  as well as the release boundary, permitted installations, consumer rights,
  warranty, liability, termination,
  and the enforceability and language of these terms in the intended markets.
- Identify the first proprietary release and audit earlier distributed copies,
  reused MIT portions, and all third-party notices; preserve the applicable
  notices with each distribution.
- The current draft is publicly accessible in glint-releases. The build copies
  the canonical EULA and LICENSE into the app's Legal resources for offline
  reading under Settings → About → License & EULA. Publication mirrors those
  same texts to the public repository. This access is not acceptance or legal
  approval; review the final terms, present them and obtain agreement where
  required, including a suitable transition for automatic updates. Previously
  distributed binaries are unchanged by repository documentation updates.
- Before enabling Pro, agree the actual commercial terms and provide verified
  seller/contact information, required purchase and withdrawal information,
  and a working payment and license system. Review the withdrawal flow and
  any applicable electronic withdrawal-function requirements for online sales.
  No seller address, payment provider, governing-law clause, or court venue
  is supplied by this draft.
- Assess provider-identification duties and separate privacy disclosures for
  the actual distribution and data flows, including any relevant duties for
  the free edition. This EULA is not a privacy notice or complete sales terms.
